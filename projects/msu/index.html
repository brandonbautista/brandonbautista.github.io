The Situation

Seven Months Empty, Then Me

The Department of Anthropology at Michigan State hadn't had a communicator in about seven months when I started in June 2026. The newsletter had lapsed. The social accounts were sitting there. The website needed rethinking. I was hired as a communications manager and inherited the whole function, which is to say there wasn't one.

I'm the department's only communicator, in a role the department funds itself. The scope has kept growing since I arrived, not because the job description changed, but because the department keeps handing me more: website accessibility under a university compliance mandate, media contact duties, the physical signage across three buildings. When a department trusts one person with all of that, it's worth noticing what that person does with it.

The Strategy

Systems Before Volume

I built the operating layer first. Before chasing volume, I put down the systems that let one person run all of this without it running them: a content playbook, a story intake process, house style, an interview guide, and routing rules for sensitive material. A few months in, I got through the busiest week so far and described it as productive and calm. That's the systems doing their job.

The content philosophy is the same one I've always worked from: inbound and trust-first. Discipline-specific stories, not content that could be swapped between any two departments. Specificity creates warmth; enthusiasm markers do not.

What I Led

The Whole Function

📧

Newsletter Relaunch

Proposed consolidating into one monthly publication, got Faculty Advisory Committee endorsement, directed a custom HTML template built to survive Outlook. First issue: 39.8% open rate against a 28.5% education benchmark.

⚙️

Content Operations

Playbook, intake checklist, interview guide, style rulings. The unglamorous systems that let one person run a full communications function without it running them.

🛡️

Sensitive-Material Routing

Anthropology comes with content most communicators never touch. Built routing rules that send anything involving human remains or repatriation to the NAGPRA program manager first.

♿

Accessibility Ownership

Own website accessibility under the university's WCAG 2.2 AA mandate. Backfilling alt text on years of archives, coordinating legacy site migrations. The kind of responsibility that doesn't get handed to someone the department doesn't trust.

🗺️

Website Strategy

Restructuring the site around hub pages for each audience and subfield. Built the first prototype directly instead of workshopping it to death. In progress.

✍️

Story Program

Five research and faculty features since August, each with separately written copy for six platforms. Email interviews, faculty fact-checks, discipline-specific angles.

🏢

Physical Brand and Events

Designed a nameplate system for department doors, now up on two floors. Floor entrance redesign in progress. Promoted and livestreamed the Archaeology Now lecture series.
